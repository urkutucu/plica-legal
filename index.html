Frequently asked questions

Where are my documents stored? On your device, encrypted. Plica has no cloud and no account. If you delete the app, documents are removed with it — export anything you want to keep as PDF first.

Why does "Insights" say the AI is resting? Advanced analysis uses Plica's private AI server. When it is unreachable, the app says so calmly and everything else keeps working. Devices with an on-device model run summaries locally.

Text recognition missed some words. Try the Black & white or Grayscale filter and rescan in better light; run "Recognise text" again from the Text tab.

How do I cancel Pro? In your App Store or Google Play account settings → Subscriptions. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the paid period.

Restore purchases is in Settings → Plica Pro.
