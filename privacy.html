Plica Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 October 2026 · Version 1.3

Plica is a document scanner built around one rule: your documents stay on your device. This policy explains what Plica does with data, in plain language.

Who we are

Plica is published by Burak Yılmaz, trading as Novemade, Türkiye. For this policy, Novemade is the data controller. Contact: info@novemade.com.

What Plica stores on your device

  • Scanned page images, thumbnails and recognised text are stored encrypted in the app's private storage (files with AES-256-GCM, the database with ChaCha20-Poly1305). Encryption keys live in the device keychain / keystore and never leave the device.
  • Preferences such as theme, language and default filters.
  • When you turn on "Lock Plica", biometric or passcode checks are performed by the operating system; Plica never sees biometric data.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless one of the following applies:

  • Summaries, "Ask this document" and extraction run on your device. Where the device offers an on-device language model (Apple Intelligence or Google Gemini Nano) Plica uses it; otherwise Plica quotes the most relevant passages of your document. In both cases no document text or image is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No usage statistics. The current version of Plica collects no analytics and has no server of its own. If a future version offers an optional online feature, it will be off until you turn it on, and this policy will be updated first.
  • Sharing. When you share a PDF or image you choose where it goes; that destination's policy applies.
  • Advertising (free tier only). Plica uses Google AdMob to show ads in the free tier. AdMob may collect device identifiers, your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), ad-interaction and app-interaction data, and crash and performance diagnostics, as described in Google's privacy policy. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; on Android, if you decline, Google may still show limited, non-personalised ads. In US states with privacy laws you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your data. You can change either choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options. On iPhone and iPad, ads are always non-personalised, and before the first ad is requested iOS asks whether Plica may track you (App Tracking Transparency), because Google's ads SDK may use a device identifier for advertising. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not shared and ads still work. Plica itself does not use the advertising identifier. Plica Pro subscribers see no ads and no ad requests are made.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Plica receives only a signed purchase receipt to unlock Pro; it never sees payment details.

Legal bases and international transfers

  • Ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); in Türkiye: your explicit consent where required (KVKK Art. 5). Elsewhere, ads are shown on the basis of our legitimate interest in offering a free tier, subject to your opt-out choices.
  • Purchases: performance of the contract you enter with the store.
  • Google may process advertising data outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

What Plica never does

  • Never uploads your documents, images or recognised text.
  • Never sells personal data to data brokers.
  • Never writes recognised text, images, tokens or credentials to logs.
  • Never requires an account.

Retention and deletion

Everything stays on your device until you delete it. Deleted documents move to "Recently deleted" for 30 days, then the encrypted files are overwritten and removed. Uninstalling the app removes all local data. Plica has no server that stores your data, so there is nothing for us to delete. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google's retention policy.

Children

Plica is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent at any time and to opt out of sale or sharing (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, LGPD, CCPA/CPRA and KVKK Art. 11). Because Plica does not hold personal data about you, most of these rights are exercised through the controls in the app and through Google for advertising data. You can also contact us at info@novemade.com; we answer within 30 days. You may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority — KVKK).

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date when the policy changes materially.
